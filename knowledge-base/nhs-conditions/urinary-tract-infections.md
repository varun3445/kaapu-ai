# Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs)

## Symptoms
Pain or burning sensation when peeing, needing to pee more often than usual, cloudy urine, lower abdominal discomfort, fever, fatigue. Children may have bed-wetting or irritability; elderly patients may show behavioural changes or confusion.

## When to See a GP
**Non-urgent:** women aged 16-64 who aren't pregnant can visit a pharmacist for treatment.

**Urgent:** severe fever, symptoms worsening after 48 hours, recurrent infections (2 within 6 months), pain in the lower tummy or back just under the ribs.

## Emergency (call 999)
Confused, drowsy, or difficulty speaking.

## Treatment
Urine testing, then typically a short course of antibiotics — complete the full course. Recurring infections may need longer-term antibiotics or hormone treatments.

## Self-Care
Paracetamol for pain relief, rest, adequate fluids (aim for pale urine). Avoid fruit juices, coffee, alcohol. Prevention: proper hygiene, cotton underwear, peeing promptly after sexual activity.
