# Falsified Mounjaro KwikPen 15mg Pre-Filled Pens

The MHRA identified counterfeit versions of Mounjaro (tirzepatide) KwikPen 15mg injections distributed through a single pharmacy clinic. Affected batch number: D873576. The dose knob came off while in use in nearly all identified cases. Sterility cannot be guaranteed for these counterfeit pens, creating infection risks.

## Patient Guidance

- Verify the batch number on your pen
- If your batch matches D873576, stop use and report to info@mhra.gov.uk with "Mounjaro Pens" in the subject line
- Do not attempt to extract doses from a defective pen
- If you have already injected and experience unusual symptoms, seek immediate medical attention
- No patient harm requiring treatment has been reported to date, but caution is warranted
