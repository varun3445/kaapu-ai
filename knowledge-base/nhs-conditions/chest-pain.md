# Chest Pain

## Emergency (call 999 immediately)
Sudden pain or discomfort in the chest that does not go away, especially if radiating to arms, neck, jaw, stomach, or back, or accompanied by sweating, nausea, dizziness, or breathlessness. Suggests possible heart attack.

## When to See a GP
Intermittent chest discomfort, or brief chest pain that resolves but causes concern.

## Common Non-Serious Causes

| Symptom Pattern | Possible Cause |
|---|---|
| Post-eating, with reflux symptoms | Heartburn or indigestion |
| Following injury/exercise, worsens with breathing | Muscle strain |
| Sharp, stress-triggered, with rapid heartbeat | Anxiety or panic attack |
| With cough, mucus, fever | Chest infection or pneumonia |
| Tingling with rash progressing to blisters | Shingles |

## Heart-Related Causes
Pericarditis, angina, heart attack. Risk increases with age, smoking, obesity, high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol, family history of early cardiac events.

## Key Takeaway
Most chest pain is not serious, but medical evaluation is essential for accurate diagnosis — especially given how serious the emergency causes can be.
