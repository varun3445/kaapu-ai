# Finasteride and Dutasteride: Updated Safety Warnings

The MHRA reviewed evidence regarding finasteride and dutasteride's association with suicidal thoughts and behaviours and mandated additional risk-minimization measures.

## Key Clinical Considerations

- These medicines are linked to depression, suicidal ideation and sexual dysfunction which may persist after treatment is stopped
- Review patient medical histories for depression or suicidal thoughts before prescribing
- Provide patients with product leaflets and educational cards
- Monitor regularly for psychiatric or sexual side effects

## Dosage-Specific Guidance

- Finasteride 1mg (hair loss): patients should discontinue immediately if depression or suicidal thoughts develop
- Finasteride 5mg (prostate) and dutasteride: patients should consult healthcare providers promptly if these symptoms emerge
- Sexual dysfunction warnings apply to both formulations

## Patient Guidance

- Read provided educational materials before starting treatment
- Disclose prior depression or suicidal ideation history
- Stop finasteride 1mg and seek medical help immediately if experiencing mood changes or suicidal thoughts
- Contact doctors if experiencing sexual dysfunction
- Call 999 for serious harm concerns

## Data Context

Yellow Card reports (1994–May 2025): 170 cases of suicidal ideation with finasteride; 5 with dutasteride; 19 fatal suicides associated with finasteride. Over 400,000 monthly prescriptions for finasteride 5mg and dutasteride combined.
