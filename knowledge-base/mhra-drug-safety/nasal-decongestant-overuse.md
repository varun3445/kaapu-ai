# Nasal Decongestants: Rebound Congestion Risk with Overuse

Prolonged use of nasal sprays containing xylometazoline or oxymetazoline beyond recommended duration causes worsening congestion when effects wear off (rhinitis medicamentosa). Extended use can damage nasal structures permanently and reduce medication effectiveness.

## Key Advice

- Do not exceed recommended doses
- Do not use these products for more than 5 consecutive days
- If symptoms worsen or don't improve after 5 days, a different treatment is needed
- Abrupt cessation after dependency can temporarily worsen symptoms — gradual withdrawal may be needed
- Symptoms typically resolve within 3 months with early intervention

## Patient Guidance

Follow package instructions carefully. Avoid exceeding daily doses or using continuously beyond 5 days. If you're struggling to stop, consult a healthcare professional rather than stopping abruptly.
