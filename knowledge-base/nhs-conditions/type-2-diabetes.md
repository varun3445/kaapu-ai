# Type 2 Diabetes

## What It Is
Type 2 diabetes is when a hormone called insulin does not work properly, or there isn't enough of it, causing blood glucose levels to become too high. Affects both adults and children.

## Symptoms
Feeling very tired, peeing more than usual, feeling thirsty all the time, losing weight without trying. Additional signs: blurred vision, delayed wound healing, genital itching or recurring thrush infections. Symptoms typically emerge gradually — some people remain asymptomatic.

## When to See a GP
If you suspect you or your child may have type 2 diabetes, or face higher risk due to ethnicity, age, weight, activity level, or family history. Getting checked even without symptoms is recommended since many cases go unnoticed.

## Diagnosis
Blood tests measuring glucose levels confirm diagnosis and distinguish between type 1 and type 2. Results typically arrive within several days.

## Risk Assessment
Consider Diabetes UK's risk assessment tool, or an NHS health check if aged 40-74 without existing health conditions.
