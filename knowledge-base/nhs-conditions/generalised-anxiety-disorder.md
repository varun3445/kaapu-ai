# Generalised Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

## Symptoms
Feelings of stress or worry that affect daily life and are difficult to control. Additional symptoms: sleep disturbances, restlessness, irritability, concentration difficulties, fatigue, muscle tension, gastrointestinal issues, heart palpitations, dizziness/lightheadedness, low mood or depression.

Occasional anxiety is normal — GAD diagnosis involves persistent worry affecting everyday functioning, lasting at least 6 months, across multiple areas of life, with difficulty controlling the anxious feelings.

## When to See a GP
If you think you might have GAD, or you've been diagnosed and treatments aren't helping.

## Alternative Access
Adults 18+ can self-refer directly to NHS talking therapies without a GP referral. Pregnant individuals or those within 12 months postpartum can seek support via midwife, doctor, or health visitor.
